# Basecamp

Bring your Basecamp projects into Claude. Ask what happened on a project
while you were out, what's on your plate this week, or what a long thread
actually decided. Then have Claude do the follow-through: add and assign
to-dos, move cards, post a comment, draft a check-in answer, write up an
agenda.

This plugin pairs Basecamp's own hosted connector with a skill for working
in Basecamp and six ready-made workflows.

## Get started

1. Add the plugin, then open its **Connectors** tab and connect Basecamp.
2. Sign in to Basecamp and choose **Full access**, or **Read-only** if you
   only want Claude to read.
3. Ask away: "What's assigned to me in Basecamp this week?"

You need a Basecamp account. There's no extra charge.

## Workflows

In Cowork and Claude Code, run them as commands. In chat, just ask, and
Claude picks the one that fits.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/basecamp:catch-up <project>` | What changed on a project, what was decided, what's open, and what needs you |
| `/basecamp:weekly-recap` | What you finished, what moved across your projects, and what's next week |
| `/basecamp:triage-hey` | Sorts your Hey! menu into needs-you, worth-knowing, and nothing-to-do, with draft replies |
| `/basecamp:plan-project <project> <goal>` | Turns a goal or notes into to-do lists or cards with owners and dates |
| `/basecamp:draft-check-in [question]` | Drafts your answer to an automatic check-in from what you actually did |
| `/basecamp:meeting-prep <project> <topic>` | A one-page brief and agenda from the project's recent activity |

Claude shows you a plan or a draft before it creates or posts anything,
and every workflow that only reads leaves your Basecamp untouched.

## What it can work with

Projects and the people on them; to-dos and to-do lists; messages,
comments and boosts; Card Tables, cards and steps; schedules; Docs & Files;
Campfire chats and Pings; automatic check-ins; your assignments and Hey!
menu; and search across your account.

## Your data

The plugin connects to one place, Basecamp's hosted connector at
`https://mcp.basecamp.com/mcp`, run by 37signals. It acts as you: Claude
sees only what you can see in Basecamp and can change only what you can
change there. The plugin itself is instructions and stores nothing; the
connector passes Claude's requests to Basecamp's API on your behalf. Most
things you remove go to the Basecamp trash, where
they can be restored. Disconnect any time from the plugin's Connectors tab
or from Connected apps in your Basecamp profile menu.
